Who We Are

GRAMIN VIKAS KENDRA, DHANBAD

Dedicated to creating lasting change in communities that need it most.

Reg. No: 625/2008-09

Gramin Vikash Kendra Dhanbad is a non-governmental organization (NGO) based in Dhanbad, Jharkhand, dedicated to social development and community welfare. Established with the vision of uplifting underprivileged and rural communities, the organization actively works in the fields of education, social awareness, skill development, and community support services.

The organization focuses on supporting children, students, women, and economically weaker sections of society by promoting educational assistance, awareness programs, and community development activities. Gramin Vikash Kendra Dhanbad believes in building a self-reliant and progressive society through collective participation, social responsibility, and sustainable development.

Our Mission

Mission of organization is to give equal opportunity to all citizens in term of good education, good health services, skills and livelihood for made people self reliance and live with peace and harmony.

Our Vision

Gramin Vikas Kendra Dhanbad wants to create an egalitarian society where not remains hunger, exploitation, disparity on the basis of caste, creed, colour, religion etc.

Our Objectives

  • To promote educational support services in rural and backward areas
  • To work for social welfare and community development
  • To support children through educational initiatives and awareness programs
  • To encourage skill development and self-reliance among youth
  • To contribute towards the upliftment of economically weaker communities
  • To organize social service and welfare activities for public benefit
  • To promote awareness related to education, health, and social development
  • To support inclusive growth and equal opportunities in society
  • To work towards sustainable rural development and empowerment
  • To encourage humanitarian and community participation activities
What We Do

Our Activities & Projects

Driving impact across education, health, environment, and livelihood sectors.

Education

Bright Futures Education Program

Providing free tutoring, study materials, and scholarships to over 300 underprivileged children in rural areas, helping them stay in school and achieve academic excellence.

300+ Beneficiaries 5 Villages
Healthcare

Community Health Camps

Organizing monthly free medical checkup camps, distributing medicines, and providing health awareness workshops to communities lacking access to quality healthcare facilities.

500+ Served Monthly
Women Empowerment

Women Skill Development

Running vocational training programs in tailoring, handicrafts, and digital literacy, enabling women to become financially independent and self-reliant contributors to their families.

150+ Women Certified
Environment

Green Earth Initiative

Leading tree plantation drives, clean-up campaigns, and environmental education programs to foster a culture of sustainability and ecological responsibility in local communities.

5000+ Trees Eco-Impact
Food Aid

Nutrition & Food Support

Distributing nutritional food kits to migrant workers, daily-wage laborers, and senior citizens during emergencies and on a regular basis to combat food insecurity.

1000+ Kits Ongoing
Digital

Digital Literacy Drive

Teaching basic computer skills, internet safety, and digital financial literacy to youth and adults in underserved areas to bridge the digital divide effectively.

200+ Students Tech-Enabled
